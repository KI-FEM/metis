## FAQ - Frequently asked questions

---

### 🧠 General questions

#### What does the name “Metis” stand for?

In Greek mythology, Metis is the goddess of wisdom and prudence. The name fits perfectly with a chatbot that specializes
in intelligent, personalized learning.

#### Why should I use Metis instead of ChatGPT?

Metis is specifically optimized for learning. While ChatGPT responds to many topics in general, Metis adapts to your
learning style, level of knowledge and goals. Metis also uses RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) to provide targeted,
relevant information.

#### What makes Metis different from other AI-supported learning platforms?

Metis combines personalized learning goals with a specialized AI-powered chat function. Your progress is saved locally
and you can continue learning seamlessly at any time. Unlike standard chatbots, Metis is designed to impart knowledge in
a structured way.

#### Is Metis only for female students or can anyone use it?

Anyone can use Metis, but the system has been developed specifically for female students in order to optimally support
individual learning needs.

---

### ⚙️ Functionality & use

#### Do I have to register to use Metis?

No, registration is not required. Your settings and chat history are saved locally on your device.

#### Can I change my learning objective or topic later?

Yes, you can change your learning goal or start a new topic at any time.

#### Does Metis save my progress between sessions?

Yes, your progress is saved locally on your device so you can pick up where you left off at any time.

#### How can I start a new topic or session?

You can simply select a new topic or customize your learning goals in the settings.

---

### 💻 Technical questions

#### Can Metis search the Internet or is it only based on pre-learned content?

Metis itself cannot search the Internet live. Instead, it uses RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) to dynamically
incorporate relevant, pre-selected content into the answers.

#### What does RAG mean and how does it help me learn?

RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) means that Metis not only uses a pre-trained model, but also retrieves relevant
information from a knowledge database. This provides you with context-related and more precise answers.

#### How well can LLMs really respond to individual learning needs?

Modern LLMs (Large Language Models) are good at adapting to your language behavior and your questions. Metis further
enhances this with personalized learning objectives and local chat history to target your knowledge.

#### Does the chatbot remember previous conversations?

Yes, as long as you use Metis on the same device, your settings and chat history will be saved locally. This information
is sent to the LLM server with each new request to maintain context.

#### How often are the LLMs updated?

This depends on the underlying models. Metis always uses the latest versions and can be regularly improved with new
content in the knowledge base.

---

### 📏 Reliability & criticism

#### How accurate and reliable are Metis' answers?

Metis generally provides well-founded answers based on the available sources. However, LLMs are not infallible. If you
have any doubts, check the information or consult other sources.

#### What happens if Metis provides incorrect information?

If an answer is not correct, you can reflect on it in your learning process and consult other sources. We are constantly
working to improve the quality of the answers.

#### Can I add my own sources or materials?

Currently not directly. Metis uses a pre-curated knowledge base. However, a feature for personalized sources might be
integrated in future versions.

#### Why does Metis sometimes give incomplete or unhelpful answers?

There can be various reasons for this: An unclear question, missing information in the knowledge base or a limitation of
the LLM. If you expect a better answer, try rephrasing your question.

---

### 🔒 Data protection & security

#### What data does Metis store?

Metis saves your settings and chat history locally on your device. On the server, this data is only processed during the
generation of a response, but is not stored.

#### Why is my data not stored permanently?

Data protection is important to us. That's why your data only remains on your device.

#### Will my chat history be used for AI training?

No, your chat history is not used for training the LLMs.

#### Can I delete my data or history?

Yes, you can delete your chat history and settings at any time.

---

### 🛠️ Troubleshooting & Support

#### Why is the chatbot not working right now?

It could be a temporary network or server fault. Try again later or check your internet connection.

#### What can I do if Metis doesn't understand my question?

Try rephrasing the question or giving more context. If the problem persists, it could be due to a gap in the knowledge
base.

#### Is there a way to improve the bot or give feedback?

Yes, you can give us feedback at any time. We welcome suggestions for improvement!
