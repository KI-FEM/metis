## The most important facts in advance:

### What quality criteria does our chatbot meet?

- Our topics revolve around studying: From self-efficacy to stress management, we want to enable you as a user to
  reflect on your behavior and handling of studies while providing tips for improvement.
- We store your data locally. What you chat, ask, answer - it all stays on your device. If you delete your browser data,
  everything will be gone as well.
- Furthermore, we offer individual customization through the use of learning types, so you can choose the type of
  responses and interactions that suit you best. If it's not the right fit, you can change it at any time. The main
  thing is that you can learn well with it.
- The chatbot itself has its own knowledge, which has been enriched with additional sources that we have reviewed and
  incorporated. Although we cannot fully validate every statement made by the chatbot, we do reduce false statements.

### How does our chatbot work?

The chatbot responds to a user query using a Large Language Model. Such models are trained on vast amounts of text data
and have learned very good conversational skills through machine learning.

Our system is structured as follows:
When a user sends a chat message after selecting a topic, a so-called retriever first searches for text snippets from
our selected sources that match this message. The best matching sections are then sent along with the user message and
our prompt to the LLM, which then generates the response. This is sometimes further edited and then sent back to the
user. Our added value compared to other LLM chats lies in the way we adapt the LLM response to the user.
