## Privacy policy on the processing of personal data through the use of the chatbot “{chatBot}” as part of the development project “KI_FEM”

### General information

The chatbot “{chatBot}” was created with the aim of supporting students at TU Dresden in their studies.
The sources accessed by the Large Language Model (LLM) have been reviewed and checked by the KI_FEM team.
Although work continues on the chatbot to improve the quality of the dialog and content, we cannot guarantee the accuracy, completeness and quality of the answers.
However, by providing specific sources, we increase the number of technically correct answers enormously.

Interaction with the chatbot makes it possible to gain initial insights into various subject areas and to gain impetus for methods or reflection.
However, interaction with the chatbot will not replace a personal consultation - in the event of significant problems, the TU Dresden's counseling services should be used.
However, the chatbot is available at any time and from anywhere.

**By interacting with the chatbot, you agree to the terms of use and data protection.**


### For what purposes will personal data be processed?

No personal data is collected by the chatbot.
The system behind the chatbot “{chatBot}” automatically collects data and information about the system when the website is accessed.
The data is retrieved and processed for the purpose of operating the application's website and developing and improving the tools.

The website includes a chatbot, which is used to support students in STEM subjects.
The chatbot is an AI-based application that deals with topics related to studying, provides learning support and conveys content.
However, using the chatbot does not oblige you to participate in the “KI_FEM” project, which is why no personal data is collected by the tool when using and interacting with the tool.

### What is the legal basis for the processing of personal data?

The legal basis for data processing for the above-mentioned purposes is Art. 6 para. 1 subpara. 1 lit. a and, if applicable, Art. 9 para. 2 lit. a GDPR (consent).


### Which personal data is processed?

Personal data (IP address) is only processed if this is necessary for the purpose of operating the website, including its security.

It is up to the user to decide what information is entered during the chat.
It is recommended not to disclose any sensitive data.


### How is personal data processed and how long is it stored?

The data collection is not personal.
This means that no conclusions can be drawn about specific individuals based on the data collected.

However, questions and answers provided by users can be used to draw conclusions about natural persons.
It is up to the user to decide what information to disclose.
User input is stored at TU Dresden for analysis and development purposes for the entire duration of the project.
The entries are cataloged by numbering, but not combined with data to allow conclusions to be drawn about specific persons.


#### Technical implementation

When you access this or other Internet pages, you transmit data to our web server via your Internet browser.

The following data is recorded in a log file during an ongoing connection for communication between your internet browser and our web server:

- IP address of the accessing device
- Date and time of access
- Name and URL of the retrieved file
- Access status (requested file transferred, not found, etc.)
- Identification data of the browser and operating system used (if transmitted by the requesting web browser)
- Conversation histories

The data in this log file is processed as follows:

- The log entries are continuously and automatically evaluated in order to detect attacks on the web servers and to be able to react accordingly.
- In individual cases, i.e. reported malfunctions, errors and security incidents, a manual analysis is carried out.


### Is personal data transmitted to recipients?

TU Dresden uses the Gesellschaft für wissenschaftliche Datenverarbeitung mbH Göttingen (GWDG) as a processor for the provision of the chatbot.


### Will personal data be published?

The research results will be published in the usual scientific form.
We guarantee that no conclusions can be drawn about your person from the publications.

Further publications of project results will be presented in a suitable format; here, too, we guarantee that no conclusions can be drawn about your person from the publications.


### What rights do data subjects have in principle?

#### Voluntariness and revocation (Art. 7 para. 3 GDPR)

The provision of personal data is voluntary.
Consent can be refused or revoked at any time informally and without giving reasons to the data controller.
Your personal data will then no longer be processed and will be deleted immediately, unless the realization of the research objectives would probably be impossible or seriously impaired by this and there is a legal basis other than consent for the processing (in particular § 12 para. 1 SächsDSDG).

#### Right to information (Art. 15 GDPR)

You have the right to request information about the personal data processed about you and the possible recipients of this data at any time.
You are entitled to a response within one month of receipt of the request for information.

#### Right to rectification, erasure and restriction (Art. 16-18 GDPR)

You can request the correction or deletion of your personal data or the restriction of processing from TU Dresden at any time.

#### Right to data portability (Art. 20 GDPR)

You can request that the controller transmit your personal data to you in machine-readable form.
Alternatively, you may request the direct transfer of the personal data you have provided to another controller, insofar as this is possible.

#### Right to lodge a complaint (Art. 77 GDPR)

You can contact the data protection officer of TU Dresden (see above) at any time and, in the event of a complaint under Art. 77 GDPR, you can contact a supervisory authority for data protection.
